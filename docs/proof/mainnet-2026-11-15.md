## Risks (state them on the page when the pilot runs — plan §8.5)

- **Redemption:** a 250–300% minimum trove ranks 4th of 5 on mainnet with
  about 13,813 MUSD ahead (2 troves of ~2k plus a ~9.8k trove). Low but not
  zero; the lab's testnet V3 trove was redeemed within minutes at a lower
  NICR. Monitor with `radar.py` and Watch; `claimCollateral` is in the
  production contract.
- **Minimum debt:** 1,800 MUSD net, so about $5–6k of real BTC is locked;
  repay is bounded by the minimum net debt.
- **Unaudited** contracts and a known-limited review; Mezo proxies can
  upgrade; the Oct 5 2026 veBTC incident is context for protocol risk.
- **Facilitator:** mainnet settle untested; operator unverified; fee
  unpublished.
- **Gas economics:** about $0.47 per 5M-gas tx makes micro-payments
  uneconomic without refill chunks.
- **Hot keys:** agent keys are theft surfaces up to budget; the pilot cap
  bounds this.
